How our bylines work

The author did the testing or research and wrote the page. The fact-checker independently verified the claims — for legal and tax content that is always Charlotte Wilson; for payout, cashier and RTP claims it is Nikau Te Aho. Both names appear at the top of the page with the date it was last updated.

If a page has no fact-checker listed, it makes no claims requiring independent verification. If you think a byline is wrong or a claim is not supported, tell us and we will show you the working.
