What "payout percentage" claims on casino sites actually mean

Some casinos publish a headline payout percentage, occasionally audited monthly by a testing lab. It is worth understanding what that number is before you weight it.

  • It is turnover-weighted across the whole lobby. A site whose players mostly grind low-volatility 97% pokies will publish a higher figure than one whose players chase progressives, with no difference in the operator's conduct.
  • It includes table games. Blackjack at 99.3% pulls a published figure up substantially, which flatters a site with a big live section regardless of its pokie configurations.
  • It says nothing about which builds are deployed. This is the gap our sampling fills: a site can publish 96.8% overall and still be running a 94% build of the title you happen to play.

Treat a published payout percentage as weak evidence and the served RTP of the specific titles you play as strong evidence. Our verification method is on highest RTP pokies.

Volatility: why a high RTP title can still empty your balance

RTP tells you the long-run return. Volatility tells you how that return arrives. Over a single evening, volatility dominates completely — which is why a "high payout" page that only discusses RTP is giving you half the picture.
