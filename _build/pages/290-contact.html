How to reach us

Report a casino

An operator refusing a withdrawal, voiding winnings, or behaving in a way our review does not reflect.

editor@teachinnewzealand.co.nz
Subject line: CASINO REPORT

Include your account ID, the operator, dates, amounts and any correspondence. We investigate every report with documentation attached and have removed operators from our rankings on reader evidence before.

Corrections

A figure that does not match what you found, a broken link, or a factual error.

editor@teachinnewzealand.co.nz
Subject line: CORRECTION

Tell us the page and what is wrong. We fix errors in place and note substantive changes rather than editing quietly.

Editorial enquiries

Questions about our methodology, scoring, or why a site is or is not listed.

editor@teachinnewzealand.co.nz
Subject line: EDITORIAL

Happy to show the scoring inputs behind any ranking on this site.

Media and partnerships

Press enquiries, data requests, operator relations.

editor@teachinnewzealand.co.nz
Subject line: MEDIA

Operators: we do not sell ranking positions, and asking will be noted in the review. Everything else, ask away.

We cannot help with these

We are an independent guide, not a casino and not a regulator. We cannot access your casino account, release a withdrawal, reverse a deposit, restore a self-exclusion, or recover money from an operator. What we can do is investigate, publish, and remove operators from our rankings — which carries more commercial weight than it sounds. For the escalation route that can actually recover funds, see our complaints and disputes guide.

Before you email about a withdrawal

Most withdrawal problems sent to us turn out to be one of five things. Checking these first will usually get your money faster than we can:

  • Identity verification is incomplete — ask support to name the specific outstanding document.
  • Bonus wagering is unfinished — check the bonus tracker, not the headline balance.
  • You exceeded the maximum bet while a bonus was active, usually NZ$5–8 a spin.
  • You requested more than the daily withdrawal ceiling and the whole request was rejected.
  • Your account name does not match your ID or your payment method.

If none of those applies and support cannot give you a specific reason after 72 hours, that is a genuine complaint and we want to hear about it.

Getting help with gambling

We are not a support service and cannot provide counselling. If gambling has stopped being entertainment, these are free, confidential and available 24 hours a day in New Zealand:

  • Gambling Helpline — 0800 654 655, or free text 8006
  • Problem Gambling Foundation — 0800 664 262, free face-to-face and online counselling, including kaupapa Māori, Pasifika and Asian services
  • Need to Talk — call or text 1737 to reach a trained counsellor any time
  • Safer Gambling Aotearoa — information and self-help tools

Help is available to family and whānau as well as to the person gambling, and you do not need to be in crisis to call.
