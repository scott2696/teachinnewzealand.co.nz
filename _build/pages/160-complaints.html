Before you complain: rule yourself out

Most disputes we are sent turn out to be one of five things the player did not know about. Check these first, because a complaint that runs into one of them wastes weeks.

  • Verification is incomplete. The most common cause of a stalled withdrawal by a wide margin. Ask support to name the specific outstanding document.
  • Bonus wagering is unfinished. Check the bonus tracker in your account, not the headline balance.
  • You exceeded the max bet while wagering. Usually NZ$5–8 a spin. One spin above it, including on autoplay, can void the bonus and everything won from it. This is enforceable and you will not win it.
  • You requested more than the daily ceiling. Several sites reject the whole request rather than queueing the excess.
  • The account name does not match your ID or payment method. Non-negotiable at every licensed operator.

If none of these applies, you have a real complaint. Proceed.

Stage one: formal internal complaint

Licensed operators must run an internal complaints procedure with a defined response window, commonly 14 days. Live chat is not that procedure — you must put it in writing.

  1. Email the operator's complaints or support address

    Use the word complaint explicitly in the subject line and the first sentence. This triggers the formal process rather than a general support ticket.

  2. State the facts without argument

    Account ID, registered name and email, the amount, the dates, what you did, what happened, and what you want. No adjectives. A short factual account is far more effective than a long angry one.

  3. Attach your evidence

    Screenshots of the balance, the withdrawal request, the transaction history, and the complete chat transcripts. Take these now — access disappears if the account is closed.

  4. Set a deadline referencing their own terms

    "I look forward to a substantive response within the 14-day period set out in clause X of your terms." Naming the clause signals you have read them.

  5. Keep everything

    Every reply, with dates. You will need to demonstrate at stage two that the internal process was exhausted.

Stage two: escalate to the licensor

Once the internal process is exhausted or the window has lapsed, go to the regulator named on the licence. The licence number is in the site footer — you will need it, and if it does not resolve on the licensor's register, say so in your complaint.

Stage three: independent mediation

Services such as eCOGRA, and the established independent casino mediation forums, will take a case against operators that participate in them. Their findings are not legally binding on a Curaçao licensee, so this sounds weaker than it is.

In practice it is often the most effective stage. Mediation records are public and searchable, operators care about their standing in them, and a meaningful share of disputes resolve at this point precisely because the operator would rather pay than carry a published finding against it. The leverage is reputational, and it is real.

Casino complaints: frequently asked questions

How do I complain about an online casino from New Zealand?

Four stages in order. Make a formal written complaint to the operator using the word complaint, with your account ID, amounts, dates and evidence attached. If that fails or the response window lapses, escalate to the licensor named in the site footer using the licence number. Then take it to independent mediation such as eCOGRA if the operator participates. Finally report it to us and, where relevant, to the Department of Internal Affairs.

Can the DIA make an offshore casino pay me?

No. The Department of Internal Affairs has no jurisdiction to recover funds from an operator licensed offshore. What it can act on is operators conducting online casino gambling in New Zealand without a licence, which since 1 December 2026 includes any operator that did not apply under the Online Casino Gambling Act 2026. Report those; they are directly actionable.

Does complaining to Curacao actually work?

Better than it used to. The Curacao Gaming Authority replaced the old master-licence structure and now licenses operators directly and accepts player complaints itself. It is slower and less forceful than the Malta Gaming Authority, but it is a real process rather than the rubber stamp the previous regime was. Attach your complete internal correspondence or it will be dismissed.

What if the casino voided my winnings for bonus abuse?

It depends whether they can point to a specific breached term. A max bet rule expressed as a number is enforceable, and if you exceeded it you will not win the dispute. An undefined clause about irregular play or advantage play is much weaker, and mediation is the right venue to challenge it. Ask the operator in writing to identify the exact clause and the exact spin that breached it.

How long should I wait before escalating?

For a stalled withdrawal, 72 hours with verification complete, no bonus attached and no specific reason given by support. For a formal complaint, the response window in the operator's own terms, commonly 14 days. Escalating before the internal process is exhausted is the most common reason licensors dismiss complaints.
