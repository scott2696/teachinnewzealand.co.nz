What this site is

Pokies Kiwi is an independent guide to online pokies, casinos and betting for New Zealanders. We are not a casino, we do not take bets, and we do not hold anyone's money. What we do is open accounts at the sites Kiwis can access, deposit our own New Zealand dollars, play, withdraw, time it, read the terms in full, and publish what we found.

The reason we exist is that most content ranking for these searches in New Zealand is either written offshore by people who have never held an NZD wallet, or has not been re-tested in years. Half the guides still ranking for "best online pokies NZ" recommend POLi, which no NZ-facing casino has offered in 2026. We wanted a site where the numbers came from someone who actually checked.

How we are funded

Affiliate commission. When a reader opens an account at an operator through one of our links, that operator may pay us a share of revenue. It costs the reader nothing and it is disclosed in a strip at the top of every page on this site.

We think this is a legitimate model as long as two conditions hold, and we hold ourselves to both:

  • Commission carries zero weight in a ranking. Scores come from measured inputs — payout times, ceilings, served RTP, bonus terms, lobby coverage — set out in full in our review methodology. Commercial terms have no field in our scoring data.
  • We publish things that cost us money. Our sports betting page recommends TAB NZ, from whom we earn nothing, and declines to link the offshore sportsbooks that would pay us, because those operators cannot lawfully take a New Zealander's bet. Several casino reviews on this site carry warnings that will cost us conversions. That is the point.

Our editorial standards

We test before we publish

No operator appears in a ranking unless a member of the team has held an account there, deposited real NZD and requested a withdrawal.

We cite primary sources

Legal, tax and regulatory claims trace to legislation, the Department of Internal Affairs or Inland Revenue, and we link them. Not to another affiliate's summary.

We publish the downside

Every review has a cons list with real content. An operator with no drawbacks has not been tested properly.

We date everything

Each page shows when it was last updated. We do not roll dates forward to look fresh.

We correct openly

Errors are fixed in place with a note on what changed. We do not quietly edit factual claims readers may have relied on.

We do not chase harm

No urgency language, no "last chance" framing, no content aimed at recovering losses. Responsible gambling messaging is on every page, not tucked in the footer.

What we will not do

  • Recommend an operator that cannot lawfully serve you. Offshore sportsbooks are prohibited from accepting New Zealand bets, so we do not list them for betting even though they would pay us.
  • Publish a payout time we have not measured. If we have not timed it, we do not claim it.
  • Take payment for a ranking position. Operators have asked. The answer is no, and it will stay no.
  • Market to anyone under 18, or use imagery, language or placement likely to appeal to minors.
  • Hide the affiliate relationship. It is disclosed at the top of every page, above the content.

If we have got something wrong

Tell us. If a figure on this site does not match what you found, or an operator has treated you in a way our review does not reflect, email the editorial team with the detail. We investigate every report that comes with documentation, and we have removed operators from these rankings before on the strength of reader evidence.

Getting help

If gambling has stopped being entertainment for you or someone close to you, free and confidential help is available in New Zealand 24 hours a day: Gambling Helpline 0800 654 655 or free text 8006, Problem Gambling Foundation 0800 664 262, Need to Talk 1737. Support is available to family and whānau as well as to the person gambling. See our responsible gambling page.
