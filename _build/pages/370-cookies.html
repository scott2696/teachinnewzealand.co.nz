1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser. It lets a site remember something between page views — a preference, or the fact that you arrived from a particular link. Similar technologies including local storage and tracking pixels work the same way and are covered by this policy.

3. Affiliate cookies, explained plainly

This is the category readers most want to understand, so here it is directly.

When you click a link to an operator on this site, that operator's affiliate platform sets a cookie in your browser recording that you arrived from us. If you then open an account and play, the operator uses that cookie to attribute the referral and pay us commission. The cookie typically lasts 30 to 90 days depending on the platform.

It does not cost you anything, it does not change the terms you are offered, and it does not give us any access to your account, your balance or your activity. All we ever receive is aggregate commission data. If you block third-party cookies, the tracking simply does not happen — you can use this site perfectly normally and we are not paid. That is your call to make.

Third-party cookies are governed by those parties' own privacy and cookie policies, not ours.

5. How to control cookies

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies. Blocking all cookies may affect how some sites work, though this one will function normally.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Prevent cross-site tracking (on by default)
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
  • Mobile: the equivalent privacy section in your browser's settings

Private or incognito browsing discards cookies when you close the window, which also prevents affiliate attribution.

6. Do Not Track

We do not serve advertising or retargeting cookies, so there is no behavioural profile of you to opt out of. We honour browser-level tracking protection and do not attempt to circumvent it by fingerprinting or any similar technique.
