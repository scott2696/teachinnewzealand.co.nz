How every page here is built

The same method underpins all of it. We open an account, deposit our own New Zealand dollars, play, request a withdrawal and time it. We record the configured RTP of a fixed 40-title sample from a New Zealand IP. We read the complete terms, not the promotional summary. Then we score on the weighting published in our review methodology, in which commission carries zero weight.

Where we have nothing useful to say, we say nothing. Where a site has a genuine problem, we publish it in the review. Two operators are in our data set specifically because their weaknesses are instructive.
