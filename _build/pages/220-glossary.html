Game mechanics

TermWhat it means
RNGRandom number generator. Software cycling continuously that determines each spin's outcome. Independently tested by labs such as iTech Labs, GLI and eCOGRA. Has no memory between spins
RTPReturn to player. The share of all wagers a title returns over millions of spins. 96% means NZ$96 returned per NZ$100 staked across the game's whole population, not across your session
House edgeThe inverse of RTP. A 96% RTP is a 4% house edge
Volatility (variance)How a title's return is distributed. Low pays small and often; high pays rarely and large. Not a quality measure — a match to your bankroll
Hit frequencyThe proportion of spins producing any win at all, typically 20–35%. Distinct from RTP: a title can hit often and still return poorly
PaylineA defined path across the reels. Matching symbols along it pay
Ways to winPayline-free system where adjacent matching symbols pay regardless of row position
Cluster paysSymbols pay when enough of them touch in a connected group. No paylines
MegawaysBig Time Gaming's licensed mechanic. Reels load a variable symbol count each spin, up to 117,649 ways on six reels
WildSubstitutes for most symbols to complete a win. May expand, stack, walk or carry a multiplier
ScatterPays regardless of position and usually triggers free spins when three or more land
Tumble / cascade / avalancheWinning symbols are removed and new ones drop in, allowing chained wins from one spin
MultiplierMultiplies a win. Often increments through a cascade sequence and may not reset during free spins
Free spinsThe main bonus round. Spins at no stake, usually with an enhancement such as extra wilds or a rising multiplier
Bonus buyPaying a fixed multiple of stake, typically 75–100×, to enter the bonus round immediately. Legal in NZ, banned in several jurisdictions
Max winThe capped maximum payout, expressed as a multiple of stake. 5,000× on a NZ$1 spin is NZ$5,000
Progressive jackpotA pool fed by a slice of every stake across a network, growing until won. Depresses base game RTP
Fixed jackpotA set top prize with no pool. Near-normal RTP
AutoplayAutomated spinning for a set count. Watch it against bonus max-bet rules — a stake change mid-session can void a bonus
PaytableThe in-game reference showing symbol values, rules and the RTP statement. Usually the last page holds the RTP

Bonuses

TermWhat it means
Wagering requirementTurnover you must generate before withdrawing. 40× on a NZ$100 bonus is NZ$4,000 of stakes
Bonus only vs deposit + bonusThe base the multiplier applies to. The single most consequential distinction in any offer — deposit + bonus doubles the obligation
Game weightingHow much each game type counts toward wagering. Pokies usually 100%, live tables often 10% or zero
Max bet while wageringStake cap while a bonus is active, typically NZ$5–8. Exceeding it can void the bonus and all winnings from it
Win capMaximum withdrawable from a bonus, common on no-deposit offers at around NZ$100
Sticky bonusBonus funds that can never be withdrawn, only played with. Only winnings above it are yours
No-wagering bonusWinnings immediately withdrawable. Rare and genuinely valuable
Reload bonusA match on a subsequent deposit, usually smaller and at lower wagering than the welcome
CashbackA percentage of net losses returned. Often the best-value offer type, especially if paid as cash
ForfeitCancelling a bonus to release the cash portion of your balance. Frequently the right call

Money and accounts

TermWhat it means
KYCKnow Your Customer. Identity verification — photo ID, proof of address, sometimes proof of payment method. Complete it at signup
AMLAnti-money laundering. The rules requiring KYC and, on large wins, source-of-funds evidence
Source of fundsEvidence of where your deposit money came from. Requested on large or unusual activity. Intrusive but legitimate
Withdrawal ceilingMaximum withdrawable per day or month. NZ$3,500–10,000 daily across the sites we tested. The term that decides whether a big win reaches you quickly
Reversal / pending windowA 12–48 hour hold during which you can cancel a withdrawal and return funds to your balance. Retention design
Segregated fundsPlayer balances held separately from operating capital, so they are not available to creditors on insolvency. Rare and rarely disclosed offshore
ChargebackReversing a card payment through your bank. Will get your casino account closed and the balance confiscated. Not a withdrawal strategy
Self-exclusionBlocking your own access for a set period. Binding on the operator, not portable between sites
Deposit limitA cap you set on funding. Takes effect immediately; loosening it takes 24–72 hours at reputable operators
Reality checkA periodic in-session reminder of elapsed time and net position

New Zealand specific

TermWhat it means
PokieNew Zealand and Australian term for a slot machine, short for poker machine. Same thing
Class 4 gamblingPub, club and TAB venue gaming machines. Capped at NZ$2.50 stake, NZ$500 single-play prize, NZ$1,000 linked jackpot
DIADepartment of Internal Affairs. New Zealand's gambling regulator
Online Casino Gambling Act 2026The law licensing online casino gambling in New Zealand for the first time. In force 1 May 2026; up to 15 licences
TAB NZThe only operator lawfully permitted to accept racing and sports bets from people in New Zealand, including through its Betcha brand
Multi-Venue ExclusionA single request excluding you from every participating Class 4 venue in a region. No online equivalent exists
WindfallThe tax character of gambling winnings in New Zealand. Not income, therefore not taxable for recreational players
Problem gambling levy1.5% of Class 4 gaming machine profit directed to harm minimisation services
