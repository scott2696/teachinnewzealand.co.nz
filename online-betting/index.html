What changed in 2025

Charlotte Wilson · Editor, Law & Regulation

I have written a fair amount of uncomfortable copy for this site, and this page is the most uncomfortable of it. We hold sportsbook affiliate links that would pay us, and I am telling you not to use them, because the operators behind them are prohibited from accepting your bet. I would rather lose the commission than publish a recommendation a reader cannot lawfully act on.

For two decades New Zealand had a strange arrangement: TAB NZ held a domestic monopoly on racing and sports betting, but offshore bookmakers advertised freely to New Zealanders and took an estimated NZ$185 million a year out of the market, contributing nothing to racing or sport here.

Parliament closed that. Legislation passed unanimously in 2025 made it unlawful for overseas-based betting operators to accept bets from inside New Zealand, extending TAB NZ's monopoly from physical venues to online. TAB NZ — operating through its TAB and Betcha brands under its strategic partnership with Entain — became the sole legal provider of racing and sports betting to New Zealand residents. The change triggered a NZ$100 million payment to TAB NZ, part of which flowed to Sport NZ.

What it means for you, practically

You are not committing an offence

The prohibition binds operators, not players. As with online casino, New Zealand law regulates the provision of gambling. No New Zealander has been prosecuted for placing a bet.

But you have no recourse

If an offshore book refuses to pay you, you are complaining about a transaction that operator was prohibited from accepting. The Department of Internal Affairs cannot help you recover it, and no New Zealand consumer protection applies.

Payments are getting harder

As enforcement tightens, deposits and withdrawals to offshore books are increasingly blocked or reversed by payment providers. Money stranded mid-transfer is a genuine and growing problem.

Accounts can close without warning

Operators exiting the New Zealand market close accounts, sometimes with balances attached. Several have already restricted New Zealand signups. A balance at a prohibited operator is not a safe place to keep money.

The honest downsides of a monopoly: odds are not sharpened by competition, market depth is narrower than a large international book, and there is no promotional arms race. The New Zealand sports that matter here

Betting markets in New Zealand skew differently to anywhere else, and any guide that hands you a generic Premier League article has not thought about its reader.

Rugby union

Super Rugby Pacific, the All Blacks, the NPC and the Bledisloe. The deepest domestic market by turnover, with the widest range of markets — head to head, handicap, first try scorer, winning margin buckets.

Rugby league

The NRL, and specifically the Warriors. State of Origin generates the single largest one-off market spikes in the New Zealand betting calendar.

Racing

Thoroughbred, harness and greyhound. Historically the core of TAB NZ's business and the reason the monopoly exists at all — betting turnover funds the industry directly.

Cricket

Black Caps internationals and the Super Smash. Heavy in-play activity, and a market where the length of the format changes the shape of the odds substantially.

Netball

The ANZ Premiership and the Silver Ferns. A distinctly New Zealand market, thin overseas, and one where local knowledge counts for more than usual.

Football and basketball

The A-League, the Premier League, the NBL and the NBA. Large international markets where New Zealand punters compete with global money.

Online casino: the other regime

Having said all that about sport, the casino position is genuinely different and it is worth being precise about why.

Sports betting was closed because TAB NZ holds a statutory monopoly designed to fund racing and sport. Online casino had no such domestic incumbent, so Parliament took the opposite approach: rather than prohibit it, the Online Casino Gambling Act 2026 licenses it. The Act came into force on 1 May 2026 and creates up to 15 licences, each running three years with one five-year renewal.

If you already have money at an offshore bookmaker

  1. Withdraw it now, not eventually

    Operators are exiting the New Zealand market and accounts are being closed. A balance at a prohibited operator has no protection and no regulator behind it.

  2. Withdraw to the method you deposited with

    Mismatched routes trigger manual review and, at an operator winding down New Zealand business, that review may simply never complete.

  3. Download your transaction history first

    If there is a dispute later, you will want records. Access is lost the moment the account closes.

  4. Do not deposit more to unlock a withdrawal

    A request to deposit before a withdrawal will process is a recognised pattern and it does not end with you being paid.

  5. Report a refusal to the DIA

    They cannot recover your money, but reports inform enforcement against operators still soliciting New Zealand customers.

Online betting NZ: frequently asked questions

Is online betting legal in New Zealand?

Only with TAB NZ, including its Betcha brand. Legislation passed in 2025 made it unlawful for overseas-based betting operators to accept bets from inside New Zealand, extending TAB NZ's monopoly from physical venues to online. The prohibition binds operators rather than players, so placing a bet is not an offence for you, but you have no regulatory recourse if an offshore book refuses to pay.

Can I still use offshore betting sites in NZ?

They are prohibited from accepting your bets, and we do not recommend them. Beyond the legal position, the practical risks are real and increasing: payment blocks and reversals, accounts closed without warning as operators exit the market, balances stranded, and no regulator able to help you recover anything. If you hold a balance at one, withdraw it now.

What is the difference between TAB and Betcha?

Both are TAB NZ. TAB is the long-established brand, strongest on thoroughbred, harness and greyhound racing. Betcha is the sports-led brand delivered with Entain, offering a more modern app experience and deeper in-play markets. Both are lawful for New Zealand residents and both are regulated by the Department of Internal Affairs.

Why is online casino allowed but offshore sports betting is not?

Because the two were regulated for different reasons. TAB NZ holds a statutory monopoly on racing and sports betting designed to fund the racing industry and New Zealand sport, so Parliament protected it by prohibiting offshore competitors. Online casino had no domestic incumbent, so Parliament licensed it instead through the Online Casino Gambling Act 2026, which creates up to 15 licences.

Do I pay tax on betting winnings in New Zealand?

Not as a recreational punter. Inland Revenue treats gambling winnings as a windfall rather than income, with no tax and no GST regardless of size. The exception is gambling conducted as a business, systematically and for income, which IRD can assess as taxable. That test is more likely to catch a professional arbitrage bettor than a weekend punter on the rugby.

Which sports do New Zealanders bet on most?

Rugby union leads by turnover, covering Super Rugby Pacific, the All Blacks and the NPC. Rugby league follows with the NRL and the Warriors, and State of Origin produces the largest single-event spikes in the calendar. Racing across thoroughbred, harness and greyhound remains the core of TAB NZ's business, with cricket, netball, football and basketball behind them.

What happens to my account at an offshore bookmaker now?

Operators are progressively exiting the New Zealand market and closing accounts, sometimes with balances attached. Withdraw your funds to the method you originally deposited with, download your transaction history before access is lost, and never deposit more to unlock a withdrawal. If an operator refuses to pay, report it to the Department of Internal Affairs, though they cannot recover funds for you.

Responsible gambling — play it safe, Kiwi

Gambling is entertainment, never a way to make money or escape stress. Set a deposit limit before you play rather than during a bad run, never chase a loss, and stop when it stops being fun. Every site we list offers deposit limits, loss limits, reality checks, time-outs and self-exclusion — use them.
