Why there are no pokies apps in the New Zealand app stores

None of the sixteen sites we tested has a native iOS or Android app, and this is not an oversight. Apple and Google both restrict real-money gambling apps to operators licensed in the specific country of distribution. With no New Zealand online casino licences in existence until the current round completes, no offshore operator can meet the requirement here.

Everything therefore runs in the mobile browser. In practice this is fine — modern casino front ends are built mobile-first — and several sites support installing as a progressive web app, which gives you a home screen icon and a full-screen view without an app store. Spinjo, Fortune Play and Lucky Vibe all handle this properly.

One consequence worth knowing: if a site offers you an APK download to install an "app" outside the Play Store, do not. That is a sideloaded binary from an unverified source, and no legitimate operator needs you to do it.

What breaks on mobile, and what to do

  • Payments redirecting and failing. Usually an in-app browser or an aggressive tracking blocker interrupting the 3-D Secure return. Open the site in your main browser and retry.
  • Games not loading on older iOS. Most studios dropped support for iOS versions more than four or five years old. Updating the OS resolves it; there is no site-side fix.
  • Lobby search returning nothing. Common on very large catalogues over a slow connection — the search index has not finished loading. Wait for the lobby to settle before searching.
  • Session dropping mid-spin. The spin resolves server-side and your balance updates when you reconnect. Do not re-spin; check the balance first.
  • Landscape-only games. Some older titles do not support portrait. Rotate rather than assuming the game is broken.

Mobile pokies: frequently asked questions

Is there a pokies app for New Zealand?

No native apps from any of the sixteen sites we tested. Apple and Google restrict real-money gambling apps to operators licensed in the country of distribution, and no New Zealand online casino licences exist yet. Everything runs in the mobile browser, and several sites support installing as a progressive web app for a home screen icon and full-screen view.

Should I download a casino APK?

No. An APK offered outside the Play Store is a sideloaded binary from an unverified source, running with whatever permissions it requests, on the device you do your banking from. No legitimate operator requires it. If a site pushes you toward one, treat that as a reason to leave.

How much data do online pokies use?

Around 40 MB per hour playing pokies, most of which is the initial game asset load, so staying on one title costs less than browsing between many. Live dealer tables are video and use roughly 1.2 GB per hour, which will consume a mobile plan quickly. Browsing a large lobby runs about 15 MB per few minutes, mostly thumbnails.

Do pokies work on an older phone?

Yes. On a 2019 mid-range Android over 4G, lobbies loaded in 1.8 to 4.6 seconds and games launched in around 2.5 seconds across every site we tested. The bottleneck is the lobby, not the game. Sites with smaller catalogues like Rivo and Smash felt noticeably lighter on older hardware than Spinjo's 17,000-title lobby.

Responsible gambling — play it safe, Kiwi

Gambling is entertainment, never a way to make money or escape stress. Set a deposit limit before you play rather than during a bad run, never chase a loss, and stop when it stops being fun. Every site we list offers deposit limits, loss limits, reality checks, time-outs and self-exclusion — use them.

If gambling is causing harm to you or someone you care about, free and confidential help is available in New Zealand, 24 hours a day. You do not need to be in crisis to call, and support is there for family and whānau too.

  • Gambling Helpline Aotearoa — call 0800 654 655 or free text 8006
  • Problem Gambling Foundation — 0800 664 262, free counselling including kaupapa Māori, Pasifika and Asian services
  • Need to Talk — call or text 1737 to reach a trained counsellor any time
  • Safer Gambling Aotearoa — safergambling.org.nz
