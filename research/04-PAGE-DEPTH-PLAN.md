# Per-page depth plan — competitor outlines, gaps and keyword variants

**Research pulled 2 September 2026.** Where our pages sit today, what the pages
ranking for each money keyword actually contain, and exactly what we add.

## The problem, measured

| | Us (before) | Ranking set | Reference (junctionworx.nz) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage | 8,407 words · 22 H2 · 19 H3 · 18 FAQ | 8,000–14,000 | 19,000 · 21 H2 · 60+ H3 · 14 FAQ |
| Median category page | **1,413 words · 6 H2 · 2 H3 · 4 FAQ** | 4,200–9,000 | — |

The homepage competes. **Every category page is 3–6× too thin.** A 1,300-word
page with two H3s cannot rank against a 4,200-word page with sixteen, regardless
of how good the data in it is.

## What the reference site does that we did not

`junctionworx.nz` runs ~19,000 words on one page. The transferable moves:

1. **Five named specialists, each signing their own subsection** with a callout
   and takeaways. We have three authors and the `take` component — but we were
   using it 2–3 times per page, not 8–10.
2. **A published weighting rubric with percentages**, stated twice (table +
   narrative). We have this on `/how-we-review/`; it needs restating in situ.
3. **Game-type glossary as a single table** — 13 categories with RTP range,
   house edge, skill element and minimum stake.
4. **Studio explainer** mapping 10 suppliers to specialities.
5. **Payment rail breakdown** — 13 methods, deposit/withdrawal speed, minimum.
6. **Per-operator narrative review on the ranking page itself**, not only on the
   review page — established year, licence, bonus, game count, min deposit,
   withdrawal window, support channels, 2 paragraphs, 3 named drawbacks.
7. **Mandatory pre-ranking filters** listed explicitly (what disqualifies before
   scoring even starts).
8. **A "how we compare to NZ venues" section** — the Class 4 comparison, which we
   already own and should surface on more pages.
