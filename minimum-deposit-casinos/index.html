Making NZ$20 last

  1. Spin at NZ$0.10 to NZ$0.20

    NZ$20 at NZ$0.20 gives you 100 spins. At NZ$1 it gives you 20, which is not enough for any modern pokie's bonus round to have a fair chance of triggering.

  2. Choose low volatility

    Starburst, Blood Suckers, Starmania. These drip rather than drought-and-flood, which is what a NZ$20 bankroll needs to survive.

    If the floor is NZ$30 and you deposit NZ$20, any balance under NZ$30 cannot leave. Choose a site with a NZ$20 floor instead.

  4. Think hard about the bonus

    A NZ$20 deposit with a NZ$20 bonus at 40× means NZ$800 of turnover. On NZ$40 of funds at 96% RTP, reaching NZ$800 of turnover is unlikely. Declining is often correct at this size.

    The single most useful thing on this page. Small deposits only stay small if something enforces it.
