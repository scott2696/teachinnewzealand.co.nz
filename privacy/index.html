Privacy Policy

How we handle personal information, written to comply with the Privacy Act 2020 and to be readable by an actual person.

Privacy Act 2020No accounts, no loginsUpdated 2 Sep 2026
Written by Charlotte Wilson · Updated 2 September 2026

1. Summary

We do not run user accounts and we do not ask you to register. The personal information we hold is limited to what you send us by email and what our analytics and affiliate tracking collect about site usage. We do not sell personal information.

2. Who is responsible

Pokies Kiwi, publisher of teachinnewzealand.co.nz, is the agency responsible for personal information collected through this site under the Privacy Act 2020. Privacy contact: editor@teachinnewzealand.co.nz.

3. What we collect

Information you give us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you provide it, and whatever is in your message. Reports about operators often include account IDs, transaction details and correspondence — send only what is necessary, and redact anything you do not want us to hold.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics data: pages viewed, time on page, approximate location at region level, referring site, device type, browser and screen size. This is aggregate and we do not attempt to identify individuals from it.
  • Technical logs: IP address, request time and user agent, retained briefly for security and abuse prevention.
  • Affiliate tracking: when you click an operator link, that operator's tracking system records the referral so commission can be attributed. This is handled by the operator or its affiliate platform, under their privacy policy, not ours.

What we do not collect

We do not collect payment details, identity documents, gambling account credentials, or any information about your gambling activity at an operator. We have no access to your operator accounts, balances or transactions.

4. Why we collect it

  • To reply to you and to investigate reports about operators;
  • To understand which content is useful and improve the site;
  • To attribute affiliate referrals, which is how the site is funded;
  • To maintain security and prevent abuse;
  • To meet legal obligations.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with:

  • Service providers who host the site and provide analytics, acting on our instructions;
  • Affiliate platforms, which receive referral data when you click an operator link;
  • An operator, where you have asked us to raise a complaint on your behalf — and only with your explicit agreement and only what is necessary;
  • Authorities, where required by law.

We do not sell personal information to anyone, for any purpose.

6. Overseas storage

Our hosting and analytics providers may store data on servers outside New Zealand, including in Australia, the United States and the European Union. Where we disclose personal information overseas we take reasonable steps to ensure comparable safeguards apply, as required by Information Privacy Principle 12.

7. How long we keep it

  • Email correspondence: up to 24 months, or longer where it relates to an unresolved operator complaint.
  • Analytics data: up to 26 months in aggregate form.
  • Technical logs: up to 90 days.

8. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may ask us to confirm whether we hold personal information about you, provide you with access to it, and correct it if it is wrong. Email editor@teachinnewzealand.co.nz with "PRIVACY REQUEST" in the subject line. We will respond within 20 working days.

If you are unhappy with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz or on 0800 803 909.

9. Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies are covered in our cookie policy, including how to control them.

10. Children

This site is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and we apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. No system is perfectly secure; if a notifiable privacy breach occurs we will notify affected individuals and the Privacy Commissioner as required by the Privacy Act 2020.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version and its date appear at the top of this page.

Responsible gambling — play it safe, Kiwi

Gambling is entertainment, never a way to make money or escape stress. Set a deposit limit before you play rather than during a bad run, never chase a loss, and stop when it stops being fun. Every site we list offers deposit limits, loss limits, reality checks, time-outs and self-exclusion — use them.

If gambling is causing harm to you or someone you care about, free and confidential help is available in New Zealand, 24 hours a day. You do not need to be in crisis to call, and support is there for family and whānau too.

  • Gambling Helpline Aotearoa — call 0800 654 655 or free text 8006
  • Problem Gambling Foundation — 0800 664 262, free counselling including kaupapa Māori, Pasifika and Asian services
  • Need to Talk — call or text 1737 to reach a trained counsellor any time
  • Safer Gambling Aotearoa — safergambling.org.nz
