What makes a withdrawal slow

The reversal window

A deliberate 12–48 hour hold letting you cancel and return funds to your balance. Pure retention design. Request and close the tab.

First-withdrawal verification

Almost all of the worst delays we recorded were the first cashout on a new account. Verify on day one and this disappears permanently.

Weekend timing

Manual reviews happen in European or Asian business hours. A Friday evening request from New Zealand can sit until Monday there. Tuesday to Thursday NZ time is the sweet spot.

Method mismatch

Withdrawing to a method you did not deposit with triggers AML review. Match the route, or expect questions and days.

Outstanding bonus wagering

Blocks the entire withdrawal, not just the bonus portion. Check the bonus tracker rather than the headline balance.

Exceeding the daily ceiling

Several sites reject the whole request rather than queueing the excess, and you learn days later. Split it deliberately.

The two clocks inside every withdrawal

Nikau Te Aho · Lead Reviewer

This is the distinction that explains almost everything about payout times, and no competitor page separates them. A withdrawal has an approval clock and a settlement clock, and they are controlled by completely different parties.

ClockWho controls itTypical durationWhat changes it
ApprovalThe casino's payments team, or its automation10 min – 48 hVerification status, amount thresholds, reversal window, weekday and timezone
SettlementThe payment network and your bankSeconds – 5 daysThe rail you chose. Nothing the casino does affects it

On crypto, settlement is seconds and approval is everything — which is why a stablecoin withdrawal at a site with automated approval clears in ten minutes and the identical withdrawal at a manual-review site takes three hours. On cards, approval might be an hour and settlement is three days, so the casino's efficiency barely registers.

The practical consequence: if you want fast payouts, optimise the approval clock by verifying early and staying under review thresholds, then optimise the settlement clock by choosing crypto or an e-wallet. Doing only one of the two leaves most of the improvement on the table.

When to escalate, and how

  1. Under 48 hours: wait

    Almost certainly a reversal window, verification queue or normal review. Nothing useful happens by chasing it.

  2. At 48 hours: ask a specific question

    Open live chat and ask which document is outstanding or which review stage the request is at. Not "is it processed" — ask for the specific blocker by name.

  3. At 72 hours: put it in writing

    Verification complete, no bonus attached, no specific reason given. Email with the word "complaint" in the subject line, your account ID, amounts and dates. This starts the operator's formal complaints procedure and its response clock.

  4. At 14 days or on refusal: escalate to the licensor

    Using the licence number in the site footer, with your complete internal correspondence attached. A licensor will dismiss a complaint that has not been through the operator first.

  5. Then mediation, then us

    Independent services such as eCOGRA will take a case against participating operators, and public findings carry real reputational weight. Send us the documentation too — we have removed operators from these rankings on reader evidence. Full route on our complaints guide.

Reducing your own wait

Verify before you have money at stake

The document queue is the same length whether or not you are waiting on a payout. Completing it on day one removes the largest single source of delay permanently.

Match the withdrawal method to the deposit method

AML rules require the return path to match the funding path where possible. Mismatches route to a human and add days. Decide how you want to be paid before you fund the account.

Keep requests under the review threshold

Amounts above roughly NZ$2,000–3,000 route to manual review at most operators. Two requests below the threshold frequently clear faster than one above it — provided you stay inside the daily ceiling.

Request Tuesday to Thursday, New Zealand morning

It lands mid-evening in Europe, before the overnight queue builds. Our fastest medians by a clear margin came from that window.

Withdrawal times: frequently asked questions

How long do online casino withdrawals take in New Zealand?

On 48 timed cashouts, the median was 45 minutes for crypto, 9 hours for e-wallets and 2.8 days for debit cards. The fastest was 10 minutes on a stablecoin from a fully verified account; the slowest was 5 days on a card. Your method matters roughly ninety times more than your choice of casino.

Why is my first withdrawal slower than later ones?

Because the first one triggers identity verification. The queue for document review is the same length whether or not you are waiting on money, so completing it on the day you register removes the delay permanently. Nearly all the worst waits we recorded were first cashouts on new accounts.

Do casinos deliberately slow withdrawals down?

The reversal window is deliberate: a 12 to 48 hour hold during which you can cancel the withdrawal and return the money to your balance, which exists because a meaningful share of players do exactly that. Beyond it, most delay is verification queues and manual review thresholds rather than intent. Request the cashout and close the tab.

What is the fastest way to get money out of an online casino?

A stablecoin such as USDT, from an account that is already fully verified, requested on a weekday morning New Zealand time, with no bonus wagering outstanding and an amount below the daily ceiling. That combination produced our 10-minute result. Change any one of those factors and the time extends.

Why is my first withdrawal slower than later ones?

Because the first one triggers identity verification. The document review queue is the same length whether or not you are waiting on money, so completing it the day you register removes the delay permanently. Nearly every worst-case wait we recorded was a first cashout on an account that had not been verified in advance.

Does the day I request affect how long it takes?

Yes, more than most people expect. New Zealand is 11 to 13 hours ahead of the European desks reviewing most withdrawals. A Tuesday-to-Thursday morning request NZ time produced a 30-minute crypto median in our tests; a weekend request produced 2 hours on crypto and 24 to 36 hours on e-wallets, because manual reviews frequently wait for Monday.

What is a reversal window?

A deliberate 12 to 48 hour hold during which you can cancel a withdrawal and return the money to your playable balance. Nine of the sixteen sites we tested have one. It exists because a meaningful share of players do exactly that. Request the cashout and close the tab.

How long is too long to wait?

More than 72 hours pending, with verification complete, no bonus outstanding and support unable to name a specific reason. At that point you have grounds for a formal written complaint. Anything shorter is usually one of the five normal pending states rather than a problem.

Do large withdrawals take longer?

Yes. Amounts over roughly NZ$2,000 to NZ$3,000 route to a human reviewer at most operators, and very large or unusual wins can trigger an anti-money-laundering source-of-funds request requiring payslips or bank statements. Both are normal at a licensed operator, and the bigger constraint is usually the daily withdrawal ceiling.

Can I cancel a withdrawal and request it again to speed it up?

No, and it will slow you down. Cancelling resets your position in the approval queue, and at sites with a reversal window it returns the money to your playable balance where it is very likely to be played back. Request once and leave it alone.
