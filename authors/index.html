Responsible gambling — play it safe, Kiwi

Gambling is entertainment, never a way to make money or escape stress. Set a deposit limit before you play rather than during a bad run, never chase a loss, and stop when it stops being fun. Every site we list offers deposit limits, loss limits, reality checks, time-outs and self-exclusion — use them.

If gambling is causing harm to you or someone you care about, free and confidential help is available in New Zealand, 24 hours a day. You do not need to be in crisis to call, and support is there for family and whānau too.

  • Gambling Helpline Aotearoa — call 0800 654 655 or free text 8006
  • Problem Gambling Foundation — 0800 664 262, free counselling including kaupapa Māori, Pasifika and Asian services
  • Need to Talk — call or text 1737 to reach a trained counsellor any time
  • Safer Gambling Aotearoa — safergambling.org.nz
